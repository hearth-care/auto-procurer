4 · Calls and transcripts

Transcripts already arrive in the operator's Phone log › Transcripts folder, one Google Doc per call, named like "2026-10-31 1145 Outbound 01865000001 [call id]". Each starts with the direction, the number, who answered, the length, a caller type (for example "Supplier — Sam from Example Co"), a summary and any follow-up promised. Recordings stay in 3CX. Because every firm's number is on its job tab and in the black book, Gareth can tell which firm each call was with, and after a round of calls you only check his summary.

Steps
1 · Transcript appearsIn Phone log › Transcripts, as today
2 · MatchNumber in the title → firm (job tabs first, then black book). Short local numbers like 852202 get 01822 added. Everything else is left unread
3 · LinkA copy goes into the job's folder under the firm's name. The original stays where it is
4 · ReadUses the summary and follow-up to fill outcome, price, dates, email. Shows you before writing
5 · RecordJob tab row: stage, price, last contact, a "Calls" link. Log line
6 · If they winTheir call copies move into their supplier folder with the quote

Resident and family calls sit in the same folder, so Gareth only opens a transcript whose number belongs to a firm he's dealing with. Anything unmatched outbound to a business number he asks you about in Chat; inbound calls from unknown numbers he ignores.

5 · Following the breadcrumbs

Every step is a link, so from a chat message you can walk down to the original recording or the signed quote, and back up again.
